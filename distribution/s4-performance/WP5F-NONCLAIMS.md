# S4-WP5F non-claims

WP5F admits one exact, bounded, untimed role for the frozen S4 corpus. It does
not claim:

- any runtime, compile-time, latency, throughput, speedup, or performance
  result;
- parity with or leadership over C, C++, Rust, LLVM, or another system;
- controlled-host, measurement-runner, raw-sample, or threshold admission;
- language coverage beyond the four frozen kernels and the sealed residual
  subset;
- portability beyond the admitted x86-64 Linux ELF64 envelope;
- production readiness, sandboxing, seed-debt removal, P2/P3, or
  Nauxogenesis;
- that local untracked evidence is accepted public project history.

`untimed-naux-residual-admitted` means only that the exact four artifacts meet
the original residual-role contract and complete the independent, clock-free
WP5E process replay. Every performance claim remains forbidden.
