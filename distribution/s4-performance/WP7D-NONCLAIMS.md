# S4-WP7D non-claims

WP7D admits only a clock-free bundle replay and threshold-candidate law. It
does not claim:

- that an eligible controlled WP7C bundle currently exists;
- that synthetic tests are measurements;
- that a threshold candidate is a tracked or public performance claim;
- that NAUX beats C, C++, Rust, LLVM, or any other implementation;
- that one favorable competitiveness kernel and a different favorable
  differentiation kernel may be combined;
- production readiness, sandboxing, portability, seed independence, P2/P3,
  or Nauxogenesis.

Missing, noisy, mutated, incomplete, or authority-mismatched bundles fail
closed and cannot be repaired by dropping or replacing samples.
