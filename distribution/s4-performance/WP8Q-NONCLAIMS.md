# S4-WP8Q non-claims

WP8Q records a reviewed public-CI observation for one exact commit. It does
not cryptographically attest GitHub, acquire or publish a benchmark bundle,
repeat a timing run, evaluate a threshold, request a claim, approve wording,
or admit a performance statement.

The eligible-bundle, exact-request, and distinct-approval blockers remain
open. Language-wide speedup, compiler leadership, production performance,
and extrapolation remain forbidden by WP8P.
