# S4-WP5E non-claims

WP5E proves a narrow fresh-process boundary. It does **not** claim:

- benchmark timing, latency, throughput, speedup, or C/C++ competitiveness;
- `naux-residual` role admission;
- hardware instruction-count parity or cycle-count parity;
- whole-program formal verification of Linux, the CPU, or the Rust seed;
- P2, P3, Nauxogenesis, self-hosting, or removal of seed debt;
- portability beyond the admitted x86-64 Linux ET_EXEC envelope;
- a production, security, or sandbox boundary;
- that terminal counters alone prove work independently of the sealed WP5B–D
  structural correspondence;
- that a safety timeout is timing evidence;
- permission for WP5D tooling to execute generated artifacts.

The admitted claim is only:

> Four frozen S4 residual artifacts independently reconstruct and complete in
> fresh processes with exact checksum and terminal work-state parity, without
> VM, JIT, libc, or system linker, under an explicitly untimed protocol.
