# S4-WP8C non-claims

WP8C admits one exact register-residency candidate plan and its bounded semantic
replay evidence. It does not admit encoded candidate bytes, native execution,
a benchmark rerun, a changed threshold, a speedup, or a public performance
claim.

The fixed `r12` assignment is not a general register allocator. The replay is
finite translation validation over four frozen kernels, not an infinite-domain
proof and not production, portability, sandbox, P2, P3, or Nauxogenesis
authority.
