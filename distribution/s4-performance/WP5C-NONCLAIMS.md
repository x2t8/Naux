# S4-WP5C non-claims

The WP5C evidence admits only deterministic residual-to-Machine-IR lowering
and structural correspondence for the four frozen S4 programs.

It does not claim:

- x86-64 target lowering or encoding;
- a linker-free ELF64 residual artifact;
- fresh-process execution or checksum parity;
- the `naux-residual` benchmark role;
- runtime, compile-time, latency, throughput, speedup, or performance
  leadership;
- general language coverage outside the closed WP5C instruction and type
  envelope;
- production readiness, sandboxing, seed-debt removal, P2/P3, or
  Nauxogenesis.

Unsupported stack, type, ownership, control-flow, mapping, and identity shapes
remain hard failures. All clocks remain forbidden.
