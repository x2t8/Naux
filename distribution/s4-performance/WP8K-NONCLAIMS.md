# S4-WP8K non-claims

WP8K admits a runner, not a measurement result. It does not claim that the
register-residency candidate is faster, slower, stable, representative, or
eligible to replace the retained baseline.

Raw bundles remain claim-free inputs. A later evidence authority must replay a
complete eligible-host bundle, compare it against the retained roles under one
declared law, and admit or reject any threshold separately.
