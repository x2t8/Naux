# S4-WP8O non-claims

WP8O freezes an evaluation law; it does not provide a measured bundle. Static
validation does not inspect a host, read a clock, build, or execute code.

A passing paired threshold candidate is not a public speed claim, proof that
register residency helps other programs, or evidence about platforms beyond
the four sealed Linux x86-64 kernels. Later claim authority remains required.

The one-sided sign tail describes paired direction under the frozen law. It is
not a general population model, confidence interval, or license to omit failed
kernels, ties, samples, or environmental limitations.
