# S4-WP3 non-claims

S4-WP3 does not establish that NAUX is faster than C, C++, Rust, LLVM, or any
other implementation. It admits no latency, throughput, compilation-time,
startup-time, memory, energy, or code-size performance claim.

The carrier records deterministic code-shape and execution-path facts, but it
does not sample a clock. The recorded machine-code byte counts are provenance
facts for this carrier, not a code-size comparison.

No result from an ordinary developer machine or hosted CI runner becomes
performance evidence. Performance measurement remains blocked until a later
work package defines and passes a controlled-host protocol derived from the
accepted S4 authority and reference baselines.
