# S4-WP8H non-claims

WP8H admits one exact untimed candidate role for four sealed Linux x86-64
artifacts. It does not replace WP5F, change the selected compiler target,
measure runtime, claim speedup, compare against C, alter a threshold, or admit
controlled-host evidence.

It also makes no portability, security, sandboxing, production-readiness, or
general-language-performance claim. `untimed-register-residency-candidate-admitted`
means only that the exact WP8G artifact set satisfies its isolated role
contract and repeats the already sealed fresh-process parity gate.
